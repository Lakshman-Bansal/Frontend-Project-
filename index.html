About India

A Land of Wonders

India is a subcontinent of breathtaking diversity — ancient civilisations, living traditions, extraordinary landscapes, and warm-hearted people. Whether you seek spiritual journeys, wildlife adventures, or culinary delights, India delivers.


From the golden deserts of Rajasthan to the lush hill stations of the North-East, every region offers a completely distinct experience waiting to be discovered.

🌏 Discover India’s Religious Heritage

Explore ancient temples, serene churches, historic mosques, gurudwaras, and sacred pilgrimage sites across India. Witness vibrant festivals, spiritual rituals, and cultural traditions that reflect the deep-rooted faith and diversity of this incredible nation.

🏞️ Savor India’s Diverse Attractions

Every corner of India offers a unique experience. From majestic mountains and lush forests to pristine beaches and historic monuments, discover the rich cultural heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and diverse cuisines that define the spirit of India.

🌿 Experience India’s Rich Spice Heritage

Awaken your senses with the vibrant world of Indian spices. Discover aromatic spice markets, traditional spice farms, and regional blends that define India’s diverse cuisine. From bold flavors to healing properties, explore how centuries-old spice traditions add depth, color, and taste to every dish.

Before You Go

Travel Tips

🗓️

Best Time to Visit

October to March is ideal for most of India. Monsoon season (June–September) is perfect for Kerala and Leh.

🛂

Visa & Entry

India offers e-Visa for 170+ countries. Apply online at indianvisaonline.gov.in at least 4 days before travel.

🚆

Getting Around

Indian Railways covers 7,000+ stations. Luxury trains like Palace on Wheels offer iconic travel experiences.

💊

Health & Safety

Drink bottled water, carry basic medicines, and register with your embassy. India is generally very safe for tourists.

💰

Currency

Indian Rupee (₹). ATMs are widely available. UPI and cards accepted in most urban areas.

📱

Connectivity

Affordable SIM cards available at airports. Jio and Airtel offer the best nationwide 4G coverage.
